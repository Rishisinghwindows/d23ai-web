5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with:

  • AI Service Providers: Your chat queries and uploaded document content are processed by OpenAI's API (GPT-4o-mini model) to generate legal analysis and responses. Only your message text, document content, and language preference are sent — your name, email, account details, and device information are never shared. OpenAI does not use this data for model training. Data is transmitted via encrypted connection (TLS).
  • Authentication Providers: Firebase Authentication for secure sign-in (Google, Apple).
  • Analytics & Advertising Providers: Google Analytics / Firebase for anonymized usage data, and Google AdMob for advertising.
  • Legal Requirement: If required by law, regulation, or legal process.

6. Data Storage and Security
