How to play

  • Press a dot and drag to connect every line on the board.
  • You must draw the whole puzzle in a single stroke — never lift your finger and never retrace a line.
  • Stuck? Tap Hint for the next move (the first hint is free; more are available by watching an optional rewarded ad).
  • Use Undo or Restart anytime.

Daily Challenge & streaks

Play the Daily Challenge each day to build and keep your streak. A new puzzle appears every day.

Frequently asked

Do I need an account?
No. Monoline has no login — just open and play. Your progress is saved on your device.
Is it free?
Yes. Monoline is free and supported by ads. There are no in-app purchases.
Does it work offline?
Yes — all puzzles work offline. Only ads require an internet connection.
How do I manage ad tracking?
You can decline tracking in the prompt shown on first launch, or change it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
