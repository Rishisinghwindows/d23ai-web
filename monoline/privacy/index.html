Monoline Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MOBIRIZER SERVICES PRIVATE LIMITED ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Monoline: One Line Puzzle ("the App"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

The short version

  • We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email, or contacts.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect certain device data to serve and measure ads.
  • You control ad tracking through your device's privacy settings.

Information we collect

Information you provide: None. The App has no sign-up, login, or profile.

Stored on your device: Gameplay data such as levels completed, unlocked progress, hint credits, daily-challenge streak, and your sound/vibration preferences. This is saved locally (in the App's storage) and is not transmitted to us.

Collected automatically by our advertising partner: To display ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect and process data such as your device advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, coarse location, device and app information, and ad-interaction/diagnostic data.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial, rewarded, and app-open ads. Google may use the data above to provide personalized or non-personalized ads and to measure ad performance. Learn how Google uses this data here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

App Tracking Transparency: On first launch we ask whether the App may use your advertising identifier for tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

EEA/UK users: Where required, we present a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) so you can manage your ad-personalization choices.

How the information is used

  • To run the game and remember your progress and settings on your device.
  • To display and measure advertising through Google AdMob.
  • To detect and prevent invalid activity and to keep the App working reliably.

Children's privacy

Monoline is rated 4+ and is a general-audience game. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App is not part of Apple's Kids Category. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will address it.

Data retention & security

Because progress is stored locally, deleting the App removes that data from your device. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your choices

  • Reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device privacy settings.
  • Decline tracking via the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Manage consent (EEA/UK) through the in-app consent options.
  • Delete the App to remove all locally stored game data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
